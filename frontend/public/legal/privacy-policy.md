# PolyBuys Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-02

This Privacy Policy explains how PolyBuys collects, uses, and protects personal data.

## 1. Data We Collect

### Account and profile data

- name, institutional email, user identifier;
- profile details you choose to provide.

### Marketplace data

- listings, messages, report submissions, and moderation outcomes;
- timestamps and activity metadata.

### Technical data

- app logs, error reports, device/browser metadata;
- operational metrics needed for reliability/security.

## 2. How We Use Data

- operate core marketplace features (accounts, listings, messaging);
- enforce trust/safety policies and detect abuse;
- improve product quality, reliability, and performance;
- comply with legal obligations.

## 3. Legal Bases (where applicable)

- contract/performance of service;
- legitimate interests (security, anti-abuse, product operations);
- consent where required;
- legal compliance.

## 4. Sharing of Data

We do not sell personal data.
We may share data:

- with service providers/processors for hosting, operations, and moderation;
- when legally required (court orders, law enforcement);
- to protect user safety, rights, and platform integrity.

## 5. Data Retention

- We retain data only as long as needed for product operation, safety, legal compliance, and dispute handling.
- Retention windows may vary by data type (account, listings, messages, reports, logs).

## 6. Security Measures

- access controls and environment-level secret management;
- transport/storage protections appropriate to risk;
- monitoring and controls to prevent abuse and runaway resource use (rate limits, bounds, throttling).

No system is perfectly secure. We continuously improve safeguards.

## 7. Your Rights (where applicable)

Depending on location, you may request:

- access to your data;
- correction of inaccurate data;
- deletion or restriction in certain circumstances;
- data portability where applicable.

## 8. Minors

PolyBuys is not intended for children under applicable minimum age without required authorization.

## 9. Cross-Border Processing

Data may be processed in regions where infrastructure/providers operate, subject to applicable safeguards.

## 10. Cookies / Tracking

If web analytics or tracking is used, PolyBuys will disclose relevant categories and controls in a cookie notice.

## 11. Policy Updates

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected by an updated date and in-app/site notice when appropriate.

## 12. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact [support@polybuys.com](mailto:support@polybuys.com).
